Privacy Policy


Last updated: August 2026

🔒 FridgeUp! has no accounts and no servers of its own. We never ask for your name, email or any login, so we have no idea who you are. Your food list stays on your device — and, if you switch on cloud sync where available, in your own private account. The only thing that reaches us is anonymous usage statistics, described below.

This Privacy Policy describes how FridgeUp! ("the App"), developed by Carmelo Sigilló and available on iOS and Android, handles your information. We are committed to protecting your privacy on both platforms.

📱 Data Stored on Your Device

All data you enter in FridgeUp! (food items, expiration dates, emojis) is stored exclusively on your device, using your device's own local storage. This data never leaves your device unless you choose to enable optional cloud sync (see below).

☁️ Cloud Sync (Optional, free for everyone)

FridgeUp! offers an optional, always-free way to keep your food list in sync:

  • On iOS: sync uses your own private iCloud account, subject to Apple's iCloud Privacy Policy.
  • On Android: this feature is not available yet — it's planned for a future update, to launch alongside the equivalent iOS release.

Wherever it's available, your synced data is never accessible to us or any third party, it's disabled by default, can be turned on or off at any time from the app's Settings, and it is not tied to any purchase.

📊 Analytics (TelemetryDeck)

FridgeUp! sends anonymous usage statistics to TelemetryDeck, a privacy-first analytics service, so we can see which features people actually use. It:

  • Does not collect your name, email or any contact detail
  • Does not use cookies, advertising IDs or device fingerprinting
  • Does not track you across other apps or websites
  • Is fully GDPR compliant

What we receive is a short list of event names — for example: app launched, food item added, theme changed, PRO activated. No food names, no expiry dates, no photos and no personal content ever leave your device.

To tell one installation apart from another, each event carries a device identifier. FridgeUp! takes an identifier your device's operating system assigns to apps on it (identifierForVendor on iOS, or a comparable identifier on Android) and converts it into an irreversible hash before it leaves your device. We cannot turn that hash back into your device or you — and since we hold no account of any kind, there is nothing for it to be linked to.

For more information, see TelemetryDeck's Privacy Policy.

📷 Camera Access

FridgeUp! requests access to your camera solely to scan barcodes and expiration dates on food packaging. Images captured by the camera are:

  • Processed entirely on your device
  • Never uploaded to any server
  • Immediately discarded after processing

🔔 Notifications

If you enable notifications, FridgeUp! schedules local reminders about expiring food items. These notifications are generated and delivered entirely on your device. No notification data is sent to any external server.

💳 Purchases

In-app purchases (FridgeUp! PRO and tips) are processed exclusively through the app store you downloaded FridgeUp! from — Apple's App Store on iOS, or Google Play on Android. We do not collect or store any payment information ourselves. All transactions are handled by Apple or Google and subject to Apple's Privacy Policy or Google's Privacy Policy, respectively.

👶 Children's Privacy

FridgeUp! is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from children under the age of 13. Since the app has no accounts and collects no names, email addresses or contact details from anyone, it holds no information that could identify a child — or any other user.

✏️ Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes your acceptance of the new policy.
