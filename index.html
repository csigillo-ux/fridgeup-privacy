Privacy Policy


Last updated: October 2026

🔒 FridgeUp! has no sign-up: we never ask for your email, a password or any login. Your food list stays on your device — and, on iOS, if you switch on iCloud sync, in your own private iCloud space. The only name the app ever asks for is a display name, if you create or join a Shared fridge: in that case the group's food list is stored on our server (see "Shared Fridge" below). Besides that, the only things that leave your device are anonymous usage statistics and, when you scan a barcode, the barcode number — both described below.

This Privacy Policy describes how FridgeUp! ("the App"), developed by Carmelo Sigilló and available on iOS and Android, handles your information. We are committed to protecting your privacy on both platforms.

📱 Data Stored on Your Device

All data you enter in FridgeUp! (food items, expiration dates, emojis, settings) is stored on your device, using your device's own local storage. Your food items leave your device only if you enable iCloud sync (iOS only) or create or join a Shared fridge. Scanning a barcode sends only the barcode number (see "Barcode Lookup"), and, on iOS, a few small items are kept in your private iCloud even when iCloud sync is off (see "iCloud Sync").

☁️ iCloud Sync (Optional, free for everyone)

On iOS, FridgeUp! offers an optional, always-free way to keep your food list in sync across your own devices. It uses your own private iCloud account, subject to Apple's iCloud Privacy Policy. It is disabled by default, can be turned on or off at any time from the app's Settings, and is not tied to any purchase.

Even when iCloud Sync is off, two small things are kept in your private iCloud so that they follow you across your devices: the product names and emoji you teach the barcode scanner (up to 500), and a small status flag related to the 2026 launch offer.

All of this lives in your private iCloud, which is provided by Apple. It is never accessible to us.

👨‍👩‍👧 Shared Fridge (Optional, since version 1.3)

If you create a group or join one, the following is stored in Firebase Realtime Database, operated by Google, in the europe-west1 region (Europe):

  • the group's food items (name, expiry date, emoji, list, quantity, "opened" status, and who added them)
  • the display name you choose to show to the other members
  • an anonymous identifier generated by Firebase Authentication (no email, no login)
  • the invite code and the group's data (owner, creation date, members and removed people)

The app asks you for a display name when you create or join a group. This data is visible to the members of the group and is used only to make sharing work: we do not use it for advertising or tracking and we do not sell it. We use Firebase only for this purpose — not for analytics or advertising. As with any online service, Google's servers see the IP address your device connects from.

Items you remove in the app are marked as deleted but may remain in our database. We do not currently delete groups or items automatically: group data stays in our database until we permanently delete it on request (see "Your Rights and Deleting Your Data" below). While you are in a group, iCloud sync of your food list is paused. If you neither create nor join a group, none of this data leaves your device. For how Google handles data, see Google's Privacy Policy.

🔎 Barcode Lookup (Open Food Facts)

When you scan a barcode, FridgeUp! sends only the barcode number to the public Open Food Facts database (world.openfoodfacts.org) to find the product name. No photo, food list or other data is sent with it. As with any online service, the server also sees the IP address your device connects from and standard technical request information (such as the app and system version). If the product is not in the database, the app asks you to type the name yourself; the name you type is then remembered on your device and, on iOS, in your private iCloud (see "iCloud Sync"). For how Open Food Facts handles data, see its own privacy information on its website.

📊 Analytics (TelemetryDeck)

FridgeUp! sends anonymous usage statistics to TelemetryDeck, a privacy-first analytics service, so we can see which features people actually use. It:

  • Does not collect your name, email or any contact detail
  • Does not use cookies, advertising IDs or device fingerprinting
  • Does not track you across other apps or websites
  • Is, according to TelemetryDeck, GDPR compliant

What we receive is a short list of event names — for example: app launched, food item added or consumed, theme changed, Pro purchased — sometimes with a simple value, such as which list an item was added to (fridge, freezer or pantry), the theme you chose, whether iCloud sync is on, the size of a tip, a simple count (for example how many items a grouped reminder contains), or the date and time you accepted the app's disclaimer. These statistics never include food names, expiry dates, photos or any personal content.

To tell one installation apart from another, each event carries a device identifier. FridgeUp! takes an identifier your device's operating system assigns to apps on it (identifierForVendor on iOS, or a comparable identifier on Android) and converts it into an irreversible hash before it leaves your device. We cannot turn that hash back into your device or you — and since we hold no account of any kind, there is nothing for it to be linked to.

For more information, see TelemetryDeck's Privacy Policy.

📷 Camera Access

FridgeUp! requests access to your camera solely to scan barcodes and expiration dates on food packaging. Images captured by the camera are:

  • Processed entirely on your device
  • Never uploaded to any server
  • Immediately discarded after processing

The only thing that can leave your device when you scan is the barcode number (see "Barcode Lookup" above).

🔔 Notifications

If you enable notifications, FridgeUp! schedules local reminders about expiring food items. These notifications are generated and delivered entirely on your device. No notification data is sent to any external server.

💳 Purchases

In-app purchases (FridgeUp! PRO and tips) are processed exclusively through the app store you downloaded FridgeUp! from — Apple's App Store on iOS, or Google Play on Android. We do not collect or store any payment information ourselves. All transactions are handled by Apple or Google and subject to Apple's Privacy Policy or Google's Privacy Policy, respectively.

👶 Children's Privacy

FridgeUp! is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from children under the age of 13. The app has no sign-up and asks for no email address or contact details; apart from an anonymous identifier, the only personal detail it can store on a server is the display name a user freely chooses in a Shared fridge.

🗑️ Your Rights and Deleting Your Data

You can ask us at any time what we hold about you and have it deleted. Write to fridgeup.ios@gmail.com (iOS app) or fridgeup.android@gmail.com (Android app) and include the invite code of your group (shown in the Shared fridge section of the app's Settings) and the display name you used. We reply within 30 days.

  • Because we have no accounts, we can only find your data through the invite code and the display name.
  • In the app you can leave a group at any time. This removes you from the member list; the food items you added stay in the group's data (and remain visible to the other members while they are on the list) until we permanently delete them on request.
  • Data on your device is yours: deleting the app removes it. Data in your iCloud is managed through your Apple account.
  • The anonymous usage statistics cannot be traced back to you (see Analytics), so we cannot look up or delete individual events.
